Privacy Policy

DayNote — Notes, Tasks, Notebooks, Projects & Voice Memos

Developer: RT Studio • Version 1.0 • Effective Date: 2025

1. Overview & Developer Information

DayNote ("the App") is developed and published by RT Studio ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting the personal thoughts, checklists, tasks, schedules, attached photos, and voice recordings you manage inside the App. This Privacy Policy explains what information DayNote processes, how it is stored, optional cloud synchronization, and features planned for future releases.

Current Version Status & Zero Data Selling: The current v1 release of DayNote displays zero third-party advertisements and does not include active paid billing. RT Studio never sells or rents your personal information or note content to data brokers or third parties.

2. Information You Create & Local Device Storage

When you use DayNote in Guest Mode, all of your workspace records remain strictly on your device inside private application storage (Room SQLite database, Jetpack DataStore preferences, and private internal app files):

  • Notes, Checklists & Attached Images: Note titles, body text, rich formatting styles, interactive checklists, picked photos copied into private app storage, custom colors, categories, and lock/pin/archive/trash flags.
  • Tasks & Repeating Schedules: Task titles, descriptions, subtasks, due dates, daily/weekly/monthly repeat schedules, priority levels, completion states, and scheduled reminder timestamps.
  • Notebooks & Projects: Custom collections, icons, colors, and task/note associations.
  • Voice Memos: Audio files (.m4a) recorded by you inside notes are stored privately inside the App's internal files directory on your device.
  • App Preferences & Security: Display name, chosen profile avatar or Google photo preference, theme mode (Light, Dark, or System), selected reminder tune, and optional 4-digit PIN / biometric lock settings.

3. Optional Google Sign-In & Cloud Synchronization

You may optionally choose Continue with Google (via Android Jetpack Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication) to back up and synchronize your workspace across sessions:

  • Account Identity Data: When you sign in with Google, DayNote receives your Firebase User ID (UID), display name, email address, and Google profile picture URL (used as your default profile avatar when available).
  • Cloud Firestore Sync: When signed in, your notes, tasks, notebooks, projects, and profile display preferences are synchronized to Google Cloud Firestore under your private user path (users/{userId}), protected by Firebase security rules so only your authenticated account can read or write your records.
  • Local Media Note: Audio memo files and attached gallery photos remain stored locally on the device where added and are not uploaded to Firestore.
  • Guest Mode Migration: If you create notes or tasks as a Guest and later sign in with Google on the same device, those local Guest items are migrated into your authenticated workspace so existing work is preserved.

4. Future Integrations: Google AdMob Ads & Google Play In-App Purchases

To support ongoing development and maintenance of DayNote, RT Studio plans to integrate the following Google services in future updates of the App:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Future versions of DayNote may display advertisements served through Google AdMob. When enabled, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process limited device and diagnostic data—such as the Android Advertising ID, IP address, approximate (coarse) location derived from network signals, device model, app version, and ad interaction events—to serve, measure, and personalize (where permitted) ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and applicable consent requirements. Users will be able to manage ad personalization through device privacy settings or upgrade to an ad-free option when available.
  • Google Play In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Google Play Billing): Future versions of DayNote may offer optional paid upgrades, premium features, or ad-free subscriptions through Google Play In-App Purchases. All payment transactions, billing information, tax calculation, renewals, and cancellations are processed securely and directly by Google Play. RT Studio does not collect, process, or store your credit card numbers, debit card details, or banking information—we receive only non-sensitive purchase verification tokens, product identifiers, and entitlement status from Google Play.

5. Device Permissions Explained

DayNote follows least-privilege practices and requests only permissions needed for features you actively use:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Requested only when you actively record voice memos inside a note.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Requested contextually on Android 13+ when you set or test a reminder alert.
  • Exact Alarms & Boot Receiver (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used solely to deliver user-scheduled reminders on time even when the App is closed or after a device reboot.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC): Used for optional fingerprint or biometric unlock on locked notes or app lock.
  • Network Access (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used for optional Google Sign-In, Cloud Firestore synchronization, loading your Google profile picture, and (in future updates) serving Google AdMob ads and verifying Google Play In-App Purchases.

DayNote uses the zero-permission Android Photo Picker for image attachments and does not request broad storage, contacts, SMS, call log, or background location permissions.

6. Data Retention, Backups & Deletion Controls

  • JSON Export & Import: Save a portable JSON backup file of your workspace data or restore it anytime.
  • Delete Account and Data: Use the in-app "Delete Account and Data" card in Profile Settings to permanently erase local records and cloud-synced Firestore data.
  • Trash & Log Out: Restore deleted notes from Trash or log out back to Guest Mode anytime.
  • Direct Support Requests: Contact contacttrstudio@gmail.com for privacy assistance or cloud data deletion requests.

7. Third-Party Services & Children's Privacy

DayNote relies on trusted Google / Firebase services for optional authentication and cloud synchronization (Google Sign-In / Credential Manager, Firebase Authentication, Firebase App Check, and Cloud Firestore), and may integrate Google AdMob and Google Play Billing in future versions as disclosed in Section 4. DayNote is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and RT Studio does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
